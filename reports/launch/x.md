Who paid the 2025–26 US tariffs? Using Census import data for every product and country, pre-duty import prices barely moved when tariffs rose. Of each $1 of duty, roughly 96–100¢ was added to what US importers paid. Same as 2018–19.

The exception: Section 232 metals and autos, where exporters cut prices by about 9% of the duty. Collected duties peaked at 10.8% vs 16.8% on paper (Oct 2025). Store prices of exposed goods rose by about 0.3–0.65 of the duty cost, imprecisely estimated.
