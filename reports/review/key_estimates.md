## Retail pass-through (CPI items)

For exposure specifications the coefficient is θ: 1 means consumer prices rose by the full duty cost of the item's imports. Item trends are extrapolated from the pre-period; without them, high-exposure goods' pre-existing relative decline dominates (DATA.md, CPI pre-trends).
